At the edge of Nicollet Mall, a flight of steps leads into an outdoor room. Concrete terraces, falling water and a broad basin give downtown Minneapolis a place to sit and gather.

Street edge

A room in the city

M. Paul Friedberg and Associates placed the plaza below street level. The terraced plan creates places to look across the space and places to gather within it.

Jason Riedy, 2012. CC BY 2.0. Cropped for display.

The basin

Water sets the pace

The reflecting pool gives the plaza a quiet center. Cascading fountains bring a different sound and rhythm to its edges.

Sharon Mollerus, 2025. CC BY 4.0. Detail crop.

Preservation

Keeping the public room

A preservation campaign helped save the plaza from demolition. Rehabilitation retained defining elements of the sunken plaza while reconfiguring it for accessibility, safety and water management.

Sharon Mollerus, 2025. CC BY 4.0. Cropped for display.

Peavey Plaza

A civic landscape in downtown Minneapolis shaped by water, concrete, and the movement of people from street level to the basin below.

History and preservation

Completed in 1975, the plaza survived a proposal for demolition and was later rehabilitated. Docomomo US recognized preservation advocacy in 2014 and the rehabilitation design in 2022.
